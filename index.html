Yuhao Yang

Build agents that operate digital worlds. Work across models, infra, and harnesses.

I built CLI-Anything, which reached #1 on GitHub Trending globally. Since its early-2026 release, it has led the shift toward agent-native computer use, reflecting a growing consensus that software should be built for agents. This direction is now embraced by platforms including Feishu, WeCom, and Notion, etc., through releasing their own CLIs.

I led UltraCUA, one of the first works to establish the hybrid computer-use paradigm. I also introduced Aria-UI, whose open dataset is a data foundation for training today’s computer-use agents.

I received my PhD from The University of Hong Kong. My thesis received the 2026 ACM SIGKDD Dissertation Award (Best Runner-Up).
